# Negotiation Templates

Used by `offer-negotiation`. One specific ask. No invented competing offers. The user decides and sends.

## Phone script

1. **Acknowledge**: "Thank you for the offer. I've read it through carefully."
2. **Enthusiasm, once**: "I want to make this work. [One specific reason tied to the role or team.]"
3. **The ask and the reason**: "The one thing I'd like to talk about is base salary. Based on [the posted range of $X to $Y / the scope of the role] and what I've delivered, like [fact in approved phrasing], I'm asking for $[specific number]."
4. **Stop and listen.** Do not fill the silence.
5. **If they say base is fixed**: "I understand. Would you be open to [secondary ask: a $N sign-on / a compensation review at 6 months / N more PTO days]?"
6. **If they need time**: "Of course. When would be a good time to reconnect?"

## Email

**Subject:** [Role] offer: one question

[First name],

Thank you for the offer for [role]. [One sentence of real enthusiasm, specific to the team or problem.]

I'd like to ask about base salary. Given [the posted range / the role's scope] and results like [fact in approved phrasing], I'm asking for $[specific number]. With that, I'm ready to [sign / move to next steps].

Could we find 15 minutes [this week / by date] to talk it through?

Best,
[Full name]
