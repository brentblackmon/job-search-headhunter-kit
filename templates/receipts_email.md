Quick on me: [current or last title], [years] years in [domain].

---

Checklist before handing to the user:
- [ ] Opening cites a dated company source
- [ ] 4 to 6 bullets, each starts with a bold "I've", each has a number
- [ ] Every number matches approved phrasing in facts.md
- [ ] No em dashes, no filler words
- [ ] Address status labeled: VERIFIED or PATTERN GUESS, NOT VERIFIED
- [ ] Tracker checked for prior contact and exclusions
