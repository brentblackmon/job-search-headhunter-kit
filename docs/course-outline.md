# Course Outline (draft)

> Draft only. Audience, format, length, and pricing are decided later. Modules map one to one onto the kit's org chart so the course and the tool teach the same mental model.

Throughline: **run your job search like a small recruiting firm with one client, and make the Fact Checker your most important hire.**

## Module 0: The org chart (15 min)
- Why a job search needs a team, not a chatbot.
- The three layers: you, the Head Headhunter, and the specialists.
- "Nothing goes out without my yes." What the kit does and never does.
- Demo: the Jordan Rivera sample run, start to finish in 5 minutes.

## Module 1: Intake and the facts file (45 min)
Skill: `intake`
- Why a verified facts file beats a resume as your source of truth.
- Approved phrasing: same number, different claim (resolution time vs. wait time; gross vs. net retention).
- Exercise: write 5 metrics with baseline, result, timeframe, and approved phrasing.
- Exclusions: non-competes and former employers.

## Module 2: The Fact Checker (30 min)
Skill: `fact-check`
- The five mistakes that end conversations: wrong metric, rounded number, unconfirmed claim, wrong name, guessed email.
- Walkthrough: Jordan's first Harborline draft and the five fixes.
- Bounce checks and email labels.

## Module 3: Pipeline, the visible market (40 min)
Skill: `talent-scout`
- Grading on proof, not titles. The A/B/C rubric.
- Setting up a scheduled scan (weekdays 7am, 1pm, 5pm).
- Exercise: grade 5 real postings against your facts file.

## Module 4: Pipeline, the hidden market (45 min)
Skills: `signal-search`, `company-research`
- The signals: new CEO or COO, funding, acquisitions, C-suite rebuilds. Why each creates a role.
- Dated sources only. Confirming the right executive is current.
- Hidden channels: communities, niche newsletters, search firms, PE talent partners.
- Exercise: find 2 signal companies in your field and one channel.

## Module 5: Brand (60 min)
Skills: `resume-builder`, `baseball-card`, `cover-letter`, `linkedin-review`
- The ATS-safe resume: what breaks parsers and why.
- Tailoring surgically: headline and first summary paragraph.
- The baseball card: for humans, never for portals.
- LinkedIn: the keywords recruiters actually type.
- Cover letters that start with the company, not with "I am writing to apply."

## Module 6: Outreach (45 min)
Skill: `outreach-package`
- The receipts email: their words, "Quick on me," 4 to 6 "I've..." bullets, 15 minutes.
- The 300-character LinkedIn note.
- Tracker discipline: never contact twice by accident.

## Module 7: The Head Headhunter's routine (30 min)
Skill: `headhunter-chief-of-staff`
- The 15-minute morning briefing.
- Follow-up cadence: 24-hour thank-you, 3-day value-add, 14-day close-the-loop. Never "just checking in."
- Logging exact text sent.

## Module 8: Interviews (60 min)
Skill: `interview-prep`
- Predicting the 15 questions from the job description and the interviewer's seat.
- STAR answers built only from your facts, and what to do when no fact fits.
- Live role-play.

## Module 9: Offers (30 min)
Skill: `offer-negotiation`
- One specific ask, one real reason.
- Why you never invent a competing offer.
- Script and email walkthrough.

## Module 10: Put it together (30 min)
- Your first week plan (from the README).
- Keeping your data private.
- Customizing skills for your field.

## Materials per module
- Slides or screen recording of the skill in action on the Jordan Rivera sample.
- The matching template from `templates/`.
- A short exercise using the learner's own facts file.
