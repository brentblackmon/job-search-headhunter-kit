Recruit Rocket 1.1.1

Quick start. About 5 minutes from install to your first results.

What you need

  • A Claude Pro or Max plan.
  • The Claude desktop app, with Cowork.
  • A folder on your computer for your job search.
  • Your resume (Word or PDF), or your LinkedIn PDF.
  • Optional but recommended: the Gmail connector, so it can find your job alerts and skip jobs you already applied to.

1. Install in 3 clicks

1
In the Claude desktop app, click Customize (in some versions, Settings).
2
Click Skills.
3
Click Upload skill and pick recruit-rocket.zip from your purchase email. Leave it zipped. If you see an on and off switch, turn it on.

2. Make a folder

Make a new folder on your computer, for example Job Search in Documents. Put your resume in it. Switch to Cowork in the Claude app and pick that folder when it asks.

3. Type "Set me up"

Attach your resume and type:

Set me up with Recruit Rocket. I am job hunting in Columbus, Ohio, United States.

Use your own city and country. Click Allow when Claude asks. You answer 3 quick questions (most are one click), and in a few minutes you see your first results: top job picks, hiring signals, and recruiters running searches. You pick which ones get a full package: a tailored resume, a one-page stat sheet, and an outreach email.

Every day after that

Say "What should I do today?" Have a job in mind? Paste it and say "Score my resume against this job."

Recruit Rocket writes drafts only. You review and send everything yourself. Your files stay in your folder. Single-user license: see LICENSE in the zip.
