# VP Operations, Harborline Software (fictional sample posting)

Posted 2026-08-14. Nashville, TN or remote (US). Reports to the COO.

We are building one customer team, from signature to renewal. Our next VP Operations will run implementation, support, and customer education as a single post-sale organization, and help us keep our promise to every customer: live in 60 days.

What you will do
- Lead implementation, support, and training teams of 50 or more people across three time zones.
- Own time to go-live, gross revenue retention, and support resolution time as the team's core metrics.
- Customer education is a product, not an afterthought. Build a training program that customers finish and renew because of.
- Integrate teams and customers from acquired companies onto one platform and one way of working.
- Partner with Product and Sales on onboarding scope, handoffs, and renewal risk.

What you bring
- 10 or more years in customer operations for B2B software, including 5 or more years leading managers.
- A record of shortening implementation timelines and raising retention.
- Experience with Salesforce, Gainsight, Zendesk, and Jira.
- Experience integrating an acquired company's customers and staff.
