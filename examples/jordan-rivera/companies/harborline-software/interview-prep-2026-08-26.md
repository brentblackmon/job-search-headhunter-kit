## STAR answers (facts.md only)

### Q2 and Q3: The path to 60 days / how you cut implementation time
- S: In 2021 new customers waited 16 weeks to go live, while sales promised 8.
- T: Make go-live predictable without adding headcount.
- A: I sorted every customer into three standard tiers with fixed scopes, wrote go-live checklists, moved custom work to a paid phase after go-live, and ran a weekly review of every open implementation.
- R: I cut average implementation time from 16 weeks to 9 weeks. (T1)
- Bridge to Harborline: "9 weeks is 63 days. Getting to 60 for every customer means the same tiering plus a hard look at the slowest 20%."

### Q4: Implementation, support, and retention
- S: Renewals at Tidewater were at risk when the customer's first year went badly.
- T: Connect post-sale work to the renewal.
- A: Paired each implementation lead with an account owner through the first renewal.
- R: That helped raise gross revenue retention from 89% to 95% in three years. (T2; say "helped")

- Do not claim NRR ownership.

### Q6: Leading a team of 50+
- R: I lead 64 people and an $11.2M operating budget across implementation, support, and training. (T4) Add the weekly operating review from Story 1.
