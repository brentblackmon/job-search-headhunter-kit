# LinkedIn Connection Note Template

Under 300 characters, counted. One company fact, one proof point, a soft ask.

---

Hi [First name], [specific company fact, e.g. "saw the Harbor acquisition closed in May"]. I [proof point in approved phrasing, short]. Would value connecting and trading notes on [their problem].

---

Rules:
- No links, no attachments, no "pick your brain."
- Count characters including spaces. Show the count to the user.
- If the note runs long, cut the company fact to its shortest true form first.
