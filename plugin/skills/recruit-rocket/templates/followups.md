# Follow-up Templates

Scheduled by `headhunter-chief-of-staff`. Every follow-up adds something new. Never "just checking in."

## 1. Thank-you (within 24 hours of any interview)

**Subject:** Thank you, [one thing you discussed]

[First name],

Thank you for the time today. [One specific thing they said, in a sentence.] It helped me see [how you'd approach it, one sentence].

[Optional: one short line tying a fact from facts.md to what they said.]

I'm glad to keep the conversation going.

[Full name]

## 2. Value-add (3 business days after an interview)

**Subject:** [Topic from the interview]: a thought

[First name],

You mentioned [their challenge]. [One new thing: a short idea, a relevant article (title, publisher, date), or a quick outline of a first step.] [One sentence on why it matters for them.]

No reply needed. I thought it was useful.

[Full name]

## 3. Close the loop (14 days after last touch with no reply)

**Subject:** Closing the loop on [role or topic]

[First name],

I know timing and priorities shift. I'll stop filling your inbox, but I'm still interested in [their problem or the role] and happy to talk whenever it makes sense.

[One new line of value, optional.]

Best,
[Full name]

After this note, the tracker status becomes `Closed - no response`. No fourth message.
