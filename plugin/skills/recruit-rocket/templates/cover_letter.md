# Cover Letter Template

Used by `cover-letter`. 120 to 250 words in the body. One page.

---

[Full name]
[City, State] | [Phone] | [Email]

[Date]

Dear [Name, if confirmed current; otherwise "[Company] hiring team"],

[Opening, 2 to 3 sentences. Their situation in their own words, dated, and the problem it creates for this role.]

[Receipts, 3 to 5 short lines or a short paragraph each. Each is one thing I've done that maps to that problem, with a number in approved phrasing.]

[One-line close. A plain ask.]

Best,
[Full name]

---

Never open with "I am writing to apply." The first sentence is about them.
