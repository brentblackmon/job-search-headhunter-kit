# Receipts Email Template

Used by `outreach-package`. Under 200 words in the body. The user sends it. `generators/outreach/build-outreach.js` checks every line of this shape.

---

**Subject:** [The role, plain and specific, for example "Chief Operating Officer role at Northpeak Software". Never a slogan.]

[First name], (or "Hello," when no contact is confirmed by a source from the last 12 months)

[One researched, dated fact about the company, in one or two sentences: what they announced, posted, or said, with the month and year.] That's work I've done.

Quick on me: [current or last title], [years] years in [domain].

- **I've [done the relevant thing]:** [result in approved phrasing, credited to its real drivers].
- **I've [done the relevant thing]:** [result in approved phrasing].
- **I've [done the relevant thing]:** [result in approved phrasing].
- **I've [done the relevant thing]:** [result in approved phrasing].

Would you have 20 minutes in the next two weeks?

Resume attached.

Regards,
[Full name]
[Phone]
[Email]
[LinkedIn URL]
[Website, only if one is set in facts.md]

---

Checklist before handing to the user:
- [ ] Subject names the role, plainly; no slogan
- [ ] Line 1 is a researched, dated company fact, followed by "That's work I've done."
- [ ] No stock lines or general observations ("Most services teams...", "I hope this finds you well")
- [ ] 4 to 6 bullets, each starts with a bold "I've", each has a number credited to its real cause
- [ ] Every number matches approved phrasing in facts.md
- [ ] One line asking for 20 minutes, "Resume attached.", "Regards," and the full signature
- [ ] No em dashes, no filler words, no slogans
- [ ] Recipient confirmed by a source from the last 12 months, or no recipient
- [ ] Tracker checked for prior contact, at the company and its parent or portfolio group
