# Connector Note Template

Used by `outreach-package` for up to 2 connectors per company: an investor or board member, a vendor or partner executive who works with the C-suite, or a leader at a competitor. Under 300 characters, counted, so it works as a LinkedIn note or a short email. The user sends it.

---

Hi [First name], I saw [the company's event, with its month and year, e.g. "Saltmarsh Capital invested in Harborline in May 2026"]. [Your value statement from facts.md: the problem, how you solve it, and the result in approved phrasing.] Who should I talk to there about [the role or the function]?

---

Rules:
- The event is dated and comes from the connector's dated source (last 12 months).
- The value statement is the same one the email to the hiring executive uses, trimmed only to fit.
- One ask: who to talk to. No resume pitch, no attachments, no links.
- Email address: `VERIFIED`, `PATTERN GUESS, NOT VERIFIED`, or `not found` (use LinkedIn). Never present a guess as real.
- Count characters including spaces. Show the count to the user.
