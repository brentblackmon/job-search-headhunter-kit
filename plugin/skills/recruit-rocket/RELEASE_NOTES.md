# Recruit Rocket release notes

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-22)

**Value statements.** After setup, Recruit Rocket drafts 3 to 5 value statements from your facts file. Each one names a business problem, your own way of solving it, and a result backed by a number or fact in your facts file. A new check (`generators/value/value-check.js`) fails a statement that uses buzzwords (storytelling, drive growth, transform, synergy, passionate, results-driven), has no result, uses a number that is not in your facts file, or could be said by any candidate. You approve them in one click, and they are saved under "Value statements" in your facts file.

**Your value statement leads everything.**
- The baseball card's headline and intro come from the value statement that best fits the company. The card fails its check if they do not carry its result.
- The first line of every outreach email is now a dated company fact followed by that value statement, in place of "That's work I've done." The email fails its check if the opening does not carry it.

**Signals match your value.** Every hiring signal shows "Fits value statement #N" and a one-sentence "Why now", on the results card and in the signals file.

**Connectors.** For each target company, besides the hiring executive and alumni, Recruit Rocket finds up to 2 connectors with dated sources: an investor or board member, a vendor or partner executive who works with the C-suite, or a leader at a competitor. Emails carry the same labels as always (published, or pattern guess, not verified). A new connector note template asks one thing, "Who should I talk to there?", after the company's event and your value statement.

**Resources to win.** Offer help now includes a checklist (budget, headcount, decision rights, reporting line, a written 90-day mandate, and success measures), and drafts a short "before I sign" ask to get them agreed in writing.

**Conversations booked.** The tracker keeps a weekly count of conversations booked, and the daily briefing and the scheduled scan start with it.
